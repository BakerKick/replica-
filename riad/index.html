About Riad Sa'eed

A Serene Oasis of Happiness

Riad Sa'eed, meaning Happiness, invites you to the heart of Marrakech's timeless medina. Tim Antal and Haynes Wheeler from Charleston South Carolina have imagined and transformed this refined 16th-century residence as a private home and sanctuary where joy and discretion intertwine. Crafted with exquisite Moroccan artisanship, Riad Sa'eed transcends hospitality to become a living, breathing home, an intimate retreat that illuminates the senses and elevates the art of gathering. Here, every detail is curated to invite peaceful moments, private luxury, and a profound sense of belonging in the heart of the city. Welcome to your serene oasis of happiness.

Located in the heart of the Mouassine district, one of the Medina's most coveted neighbourhoods, Riad Sa'eed stands as a masterpiece of 15th and 16th-century architecture, surrounded by ancient mosques, historic gardens, celebrated restaurants, and the kind of curated concept stores that define contemporary cool. This is a neighbourhood that doesn't just tell history. It lives it, effortlessly.

Culinary Arts

A Feast for the Senses

Ascend to our rooftop terrace where the Atlas Mountains paint the horizon and the Medina unfolds beneath the evening sky. Our chef transforms the freshest ingredients from the morning souk into authentic Moroccan cuisine -- slow-simmered tagines, hand-rolled couscous, and pastries sweetened with orange blossom and local honey.

Each morning begins with fresh-baked bread, fragrant mint tea, and seasonal fruits gathered at dawn. As the sun dips below the minarets, candlelight takes over and every evening becomes an intimate celebration of flavour, tradition, and the incomparable magic of dining under the Marrakech stars.

Experiences

Unforgettable Moments

Guided Medina Discovery

3 hours

Wander the labyrinthine souks and hidden treasures of the Marrakech Medina with our private guide. From the spice markets to secret palaces, discover stories only locals know.

From €65 per person

Traditional Hammam Ritual

1.5 hours

Within Riad Sa'eed's private hammam, surrender to the ancient art of the Moroccan ritual. Black soap, rhassoul clay, and argan oil, applied by expert hands beneath the warm steam of our intimate domed retreat. An ancestral ceremony of purification and renewal, never leaving the riad.

From €85 per person

Moroccan Cooking Atelier

4 hours

Join one of our local chefs on a journey from the spice souk to the kitchen. Learn to prepare classic tagines, hand-roll couscous, and master the art of Moroccan pastry in an intimate hands-on cooking experience.

From €90 per person

Atlas Mountains Escape

Full day

Journey through the foothills of the High Atlas to visit Berber villages, terraced valleys, and the cascading Ouzoud waterfalls. A day of breathtaking landscapes and warm encounters.

From €120 per person

Sunset Camel Ride

2 hours

As the sun dips toward the Atlas, ride through the ancient palm groves of the Palmeraie on camelback. Tea and Moroccan pastries await at a traditional bivouac under the first stars.

From €55 per person
