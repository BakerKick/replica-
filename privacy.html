This site is a private concept presentation for Shiraume Lodge. It has no accounts, no analytics, and no advertising. The only personal information it handles is what you choose to type into one of its two forms.

What we collect

Only what you submit through a form. Both forms are optional — you can read the entire site without sending anything.

Partner introduction form

  • First and last name
  • Email address
  • Your background or role (from a fixed list)
  • Relevant experience, and what draws you to the project — free text

Room booking enquiry form

  • Arrival and departure dates, number of guests, and the room enquired about
  • Full name and email address
  • Special requests — free text

A note on special requests. That field invites dietary requirements and accessibility needs. Information of that kind can reveal your health or your religious beliefs, which the law treats as especially sensitive. You are never required to give it. Anything you do write there is used only to prepare for your stay, is seen only by the people arranging it, and is deleted on the same schedule as the rest of your enquiry.

We do not collect payment details anywhere on this site. We do not buy personal information from anyone, and we do not sell, rent, or trade yours.

Cookies

This site sets no cookies at all. There is no analytics, no advertising network, and no tracking pixel, which is why you were not asked to accept anything when you arrived.

One item is stored in your browser: a note of whether you last viewed the site in its day or night appearance. It never leaves your device, it identifies nobody, and clearing your browser data removes it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may ask us to:

  • Give you a copy of what we hold about you
  • Correct it if it is wrong
  • Delete it
  • Restrict or object to how we use it, including where we rely on legitimate interests
  • Provide it in a portable form
  • Withdraw consent you gave — which does not undo anything done before you withdrew it

Write to [PRIVACY CONTACT EMAIL] and we will respond within one month. There is no charge.

If you are in the UK or EEA and think we have handled this badly, you may complain to your national data protection authority. In Japan, the relevant body is the Personal Information Protection Commission (個人情報保護委員会).

Security

The site is served over HTTPS, and no enquiry is ever stored on the site itself — there is no database here to breach. Once an enquiry reaches our inbox it is protected by that mailbox's own access controls.

No transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot promise otherwise. Please do not send us anything highly sensitive through a web form.

Children

This site is meant for hospitality professionals, prospective partners, and adults planning a stay. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 16. If you believe a child has sent us something, write to us and we will delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date at the top. Material changes will be described here rather than made quietly.
